What we do

Seven service lines, one long-term partner

From first certification through long-term operational maturity — safety, training, operations, growth, language and events, built around the full lifecycle of a regional airline, airport or regulator.

Safety First

Compliance advisory, IOSA/ISA certification support, safety diagnostics and SMS design aligned with IOSA, FAA and EASA.
  • —Compliance advisory — meeting all local and international aviation regulations.
  • —Certification support — IOSA and ISA audit prep, plus compliance strategy across multiple markets.
  • —Safety diagnostics — evaluating technical, operational and organizational gaps.
  • —SMS design & implementation — aligned with IOSA, FAA and EASA standards.

Aviation Training

Customized EBT/CBTA training programs for pilots, cabin crew, dispatchers, maintenance and ATC teams.
  • —Five crew categories — pilots, cabin crew, dispatchers, maintenance and ATC.
  • —Simulator acceptance — certification assistance for flight simulators.
  • —EBT / CBTA — evidence- and competency-based training implementation.
  • —Digital tools adoption — procedure development across departments.

Operational Excellence

Process, cost and technology advisory to streamline operations and build certification-ready procedures.
  • —Process & performance — streamlining operations, reducing maintenance costs.
  • —Cost & org. optimization — financial restructuring, resource allocation.
  • —Technology advisory — selecting equipment and tools for your goals.
  • —Certification-ready procedures — aligned with regulatory standards.

Regional Outreach

Market-entry strategy, opportunity mapping and regulatory support to enter Latin American markets.
  • —Opportunity mapping — targeted market analysis for strategic decisions.
  • —Market-entry strategy — connecting you with key local partners.
  • —Regulatory support — navigating local operational requirements.

Language Solutions
