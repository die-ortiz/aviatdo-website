CBTA Foundations, Regulatory Framework and Current Adoption in the Americas

From Concept to Early Implementation

Register here
About this session

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to Competency-Based Training and Assessment (CBTA), exploring its key differences from traditional training approaches and its regulatory framework under ICAO documentation. Through real-world case studies from North America, Latin America, and the Caribbean, participants will examine how early adopters have implemented CBTA and the lessons learned along the way. The course also addresses common misconceptions, compares CBTA with programs like AQP, and analyzes the varying levels of maturity across airlines, ATOs, and ANSPs.
